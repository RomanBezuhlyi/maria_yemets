About me

Behind the Lens

Photography, for me, is about people and the emotions they share. A glance. A smile. A touch. A laugh. The moments that often pass unnoticed, yet become the memories we treasure most. My style is natural, cinematic, and emotion-driven. I gently guide when needed, but I always leave room for real moments to unfold. Based in Barcelona, I photograph families, motherhood, newborns, couples, intimate weddings, baptisms, and personal branding sessions. My goal is simple - to create photographs that feel honest, timeless, and truly yours.
